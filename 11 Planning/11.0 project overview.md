# CS 518 Final Project

### Introduction

Your team will brainstorm a software product and develop a portion of the functionality.  The product must have user management and authentication.  You will develop two additional features.

Key points:
* You are welcome to develop a monolithic application.  For full credit, however, you will need to develop external API(s) / service(s) for your features.
* Additional credit may be considered for UI enhancements.  In particular, interesting UI features (e.g data dashboard), not just a coat of paint.  However this is not a part of the core requirements and should not take time away from those.
