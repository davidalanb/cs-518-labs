# CS 518 Final Project

## Introduction

Your team will brainstorm a software product and develop a portion of the functionality.  You will develop two new features.  You may or may not choose to use the features we developed together.  You will have the remainder of the semester to work on thie project.  In the final week of class, you will present your work.  

These assignments are group grades.  However, adjustments can be made based on individual contributions.

        - Sprint review; retro
* week 14: thanksgiving
* week 15: presentations

## Working in a group

### The good

There are many benefits to working in groups, such as:

* Diverse Skill Sets and Perspectives
* Faster Progress and Increased Output
* Built-in Code Review and Quality Control
* Shared Learning and Skill Development
* Mutual Support and Motivation
* Experience with Industry-Standard Tools and Workflow

### The bad

However, sometimes there are issues with accountability and division of labor.

In order to ensure that you receive proper credit for your work, be sure to clearly divide your work, and use project management tools (Gitlab) and source code management (Git) appropriately.

* Use sprint planning and Gitlab issues to clearly divide the work.  
* Be sure that you're committing your own code to the repository.
* Clearly credit contributions in the presentation.  Each individual should present the work that they contributed.

You do not have to do extra work to make up for work your teammate(s) are not doing.  If there are major discrepancies in work completed, please bring this to the attention of your professor and TA(s).
