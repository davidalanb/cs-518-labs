## **\[40\] Presentation**

Presentation requirements 

* presentations should take \~15 min 
* presentations should include: 
  - slides (or another visual aid), and 
  - live demo, or show highlights from demo video with live voiceover.
* Every team member must participate in the presentation
  - speaking should be about evenly distributed
  - each team member should speak to their own contributions

Presentation contents

* \[10\] introduction and requirements  
  * vision and value, personas
  * features and stories (selected stories only)  
* \[10\] design  
  * architecture diagram (internal or external API)  
  * API design (new features only)  
* \[10\] implementation and testing  
  * work status (done / not done)  
  * test coverage  
  * demo  
* \[10\] evaluation and conclusions  
  * accomplishments (what are you proud of)  
  * areas for improvement  
  * next steps

## Presentation peer review

Requirements:

* Review two other groups.
* If there are two presentation dates, 
  * attend both dates, and
  * review at least one presentation on the other date.

Instructions:

* Name of project you're reviewing
* Your brief summary of their app (overall) and work for this sprint.
* For each rubric section (the four above), make a few comments and try to identify:
  * something that you liked
  * something that could potentially be improved (be specific and tactful). 
* I plan to share these with the whole class.  If you don't want yours to be shared, please email Prof. Benedetto.
